# Publish a Group

import { Steps, Callout } from 'nextra/components'

Publishing your interserv group makes it visible on the Explore page, allowing other server admins to discover and join your group.

## How to Publish

<Steps>

### Open Explore

Go to [oraxbot.com/explore](https://oraxbot.com/explore) and click **Publish your group**. Log in if you are not already.

### Select Your Server and Group

Choose the server that owns the group, then the interserv group you want to publish.

### Fill in the Details

Provide the following information:

- **Description**: A brief description of your group's topic and community
- **Banner image** (optional): PNG, JPG, WEBP, or GIF, up to 5 MB
- **Proposals channel**: The channel where join requests will be sent for you to review

### Publish

Leave **Published (visible on explore)** checked, then click **Save**. Uncheck it and save again to remove the listing.

</Steps>

## Published Group Information

When published, your group will show the following information on the Explore page:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Group name** | The name of your interserv group |
| **Description** | The description you provided |
| **Image** | The image you uploaded (if any) |
| **Message count** | Approximate number of messages (updated periodically) |
| **Connected servers** | List of public servers in the group |
| **Vote count** | Number of votes received |

## Managing Your Publication

### Updating Details

Open the group on [Explore](https://oraxbot.com/explore) and click **Edit**, or go back through **Publish your group**. You can change the description, image, proposals channel, or published state, then click **Save**.

### Unpublishing

Uncheck **Published (visible on explore)** and click **Save**. Existing connections remain — only the public listing is removed.

### Proposals Channel

The proposals channel is where join requests from other servers appear. When someone requests to join your group:

1. A message appears in your proposals channel with the requesting server's name and details
2. The message includes **Accept** and **Deny** buttons
3. Click **Accept** to approve the join request (their channel will be linked to your group)
4. Click **Deny** to reject the request

<Callout type="tip">
  Choose a proposals channel that your moderation team monitors regularly. You don't want to miss join requests!
</Callout>

## Message Count

Orax periodically updates the message count for published groups. This is done by a scheduled task that counts messages across all linked channels. The count helps users gauge the activity level of a group.

<Callout type="info">
  Message counts are approximate and updated periodically, not in real-time.
</Callout>
