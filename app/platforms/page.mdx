### Self-hosted Fluxer

Running your own Fluxer instance? Orax can connect to it too, so your self-hosted community can be linked to interserv groups alongside Discord, Fluxer, and Telegram.

1. Create a bot account on your Fluxer instance and copy its bot token.
2. Open the [Orax Dashboard settings](https://oraxbot.com/dashboard/fluxer/settings) and find the **Self-hosted Fluxer instance** section.
3. Enter your **Instance URL** (e.g. `https://fluxer.yourdomain.com`) and paste the **Bot token**, then click **Save & Connect**.
4. Invite your bot to your servers on the instance and use `/link-channel <group_link>` as usual.

<Callout type="info">
  Connecting a self-hosted instance requires an active **Orax Plus** plan on your account
  (one instance per account). If Orax Plus expires, the instance is paused until you renew.
</Callout>

Requirements for your instance:

- Publicly reachable over **`https://`** (private or local addresses are not accepted)
- Exposes a valid `/.well-known/fluxer` discovery document
- Gateway served over **`wss://`**

Your bot token is stored encrypted. You can update the token or disconnect the instance at any time from the same settings page.

## Telegram

Telegram is supported by Orax, bringing the same cross-server bridging experience to your Telegram groups.

1. Add Orax to your Telegram group via [@InterServerBot](https://t.me/InterServerBot).
2. Run `/setup_interserv_group <group_link>` in the group to connect it to an interserv group.

Telegram currently relays **text** only. `/giveaway` is also available.

<Callout type="info">
  Channels on Discord, Fluxer, and Telegram can be linked to the same interserv group, letting
  users on different platforms chat together seamlessly.
</Callout>
